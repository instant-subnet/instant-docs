About Instant

Fast inference with evidence attached.

Instant is building an OpenAI-compatible inference network for Bittensor. Independent miners contribute GPU capacity through outbound connections, while validators inspect signed delivery evidence and score completed platform metrics and set weights on Finney subnet 46.

Architecture

Edge

Platform gateway

Authenticates customer requests, selects qualified capacity, observes timing, verifies receipts, and publishes bounded evidence.

Compute

Outbound miners

Run the approved model behind a local backend, connect over secure WebSocket, stream results, and sign terminal receipts.

Consensus

Validators

Fetch a signed completed-period platform report, calculate one deterministic score vector, and call direct set_weights.

Operating principles

Fail closed
Invalid onboarding identity stops admission. Failed or expired TOPLOC assertions cause platform policy to hold the miner from future routing.
Least authority
GPU hosts use operational hotkeys to authenticate their outbound worker sessions. They do not need owner coldkeys or validator wallets.
Evidence before reward
Reward inputs come from the platform's signed completed-period metrics and terminal TOPLOC outcomes.
Honest boundaries
The verifier asserts only pass or fail. The platform alone owns miner routing and temporary-hold policy.

Availability

Miner participation is currently curated. The public one-line installer will remain unavailable until the production release is signed and its trust anchors are published here. Validator operations should remain use the Phase 0 repositories only for review; the Finney miner, verifier, platform, and validator workflows are being rebuilt and tested in gated phases.
